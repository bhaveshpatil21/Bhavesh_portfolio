Indian websites for Jaguar and Land Rover

Responsive interfaces and reusable visual styles for two premium automotive brands in the Indian market.

Client
Jaguar & Land Rover India (via Mobilestyx)
Role
UI Designer / Developer
Duration
Feb 2022 — Dec 2022
Worked with
Agency design & dev team

Overview

At Mobilestyx Consulting & Solutions, worked on the Indian websites for Land Rover and Jaguar alongside other client projects.

Why it mattered

Premium automotive brands need sites that feel as considered as the cars, and that stay consistent across many pages and screen sizes.

What I did

  • Designed responsive web interfaces, wireframes, prototypes and reusable visual styles.
  • Converted Figma designs into HTML/CSS.
  • Created style guides so pages stayed consistent through implementation.
