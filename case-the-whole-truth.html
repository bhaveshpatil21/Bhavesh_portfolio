Making clean-label products easier to choose and buy

Designing the brand's D2C website so every product page explains what's inside, why it matters, and how to buy it — fast.

Client
The Whole Truth Foods
Role
UI/UX Designer
Duration
May 2025 — Present
Worked with
Marketing, business stakeholders

Overview

Led UI/UX design initiatives for The Whole Truth's D2C website, focused on usability, customer journeys and digital conversion.

The work spans landing pages, product detail pages and the key e-commerce touchpoints in between.

Why it mattered

A clean-label brand sells on transparency, so the website has to carry the story: ingredients, benefits and the difference between similar products. With a wide catalogue, that information can easily turn into noise.

What I did

  • Refined website experiences across landing pages, product detail pages and key e-commerce touchpoints.
  • Reworked product storytelling, SKU presentation and content hierarchy so benefits read clearly at a glance.
  • Partnered with marketing and business stakeholders to keep designs user-centred while aligned to brand and revenue goals.
