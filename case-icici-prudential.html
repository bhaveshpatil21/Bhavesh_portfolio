Internal portals for ICICI Prudential Life Insurance

Designing and building internal tools for one of India's largest life insurers, from first wireframe to working front end.

Role
UI/UX Developer
Duration
Dec 2022 — Apr 2025
Worked with
Client stakeholders, development team

Overview

Worked with ICICI Prudential Life Insurance as a client-side UI/UX resource, designing internal portals and digital experiences.

Why it mattered

Internal insurance tools carry dense information and complex workflows. Employees need to get through them quickly and accurately, every day.

What I did

  • Created user flows, wireframes, mock-ups, prototypes and high-fidelity interfaces in Figma and Adobe XD.
  • Converted design concepts into responsive websites using HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Bootstrap — closing the gap between design and build.
  • Ran brainstorming sessions, presented design directions and collaborated with stakeholders throughout delivery.
